WHAT WE SHIP: these clips retargeted onto our own character, inside the game.
We do not redistribute the motion data as data. That is what the licence allows.

PROVENANCE OF THESE FILES: BVH-to-FBX conversion by RancidMilk
(https://rancidmilk.itch.io/free-character-animations) from the cgspeed
MotionBuilder-friendly BVH release, mirrored at
https://huggingface.co/datasets/gbionics/cmu-fbx (licence: cmu-mocap).
Bones-only armatures, root motion included, NO finger animation.

WHY THIS SET: the actor demonstrably held real equipment — the session includes
Placing Tee, Placing Ball and Picking Up Ball — so the WRISTS are rolled onto a
club. That is the one thing the Mixamo clips lack: measured at address, their
knuckle line is 58.4 deg (lead) / 69.4 deg (trail) away from the shaft a club
needs, which no finger pose can correct. CMU capturing no fingers is a benefit
here: the authored grip pose has nothing to fight.
