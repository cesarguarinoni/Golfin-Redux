=== Hole 1 ===
The right side is wide; aim the tee shot at the sloping area in the centre of the two-tiered fairway. The landing spot of the second shot is crucial.

=== Hole 2 ===
A nearly straight hole. The fairway is tight on both sides — play the tee shot carefully. The area behind the green is also tight, so beware.

=== Hole 3 ===
A short par-4. Both sides of the fairway are tight, so a reliable tee shot is essential. Watch the area behind the green, which also narrows.

=== Hole 4 ===
The shortest par-3 on the course. The green slopes from right to left, so attacking from the right side is best. Missing into the slope below the green's left edge spells trouble.

=== Hole 5 ===
A long par-4. Aim the tee shot at the left hillside. The fairway is wide — swing freely. From the approach, beware the pond left of the green.

=== Hole 6 ===
A par-3 over water. Don't force it — aim for the centre of the green. The pond is a lateral water hazard.

=== Hole 7 ===
The signature valley-carry hole. With the valley waiting ahead, longer hitters should choose their club carefully. The green is split into two tiers left and right — landing on the same level as the pin is the key.

=== Hole 8 ===
A par-5 dogleg right. Aim the tee shot just left of fairway centre. The fairway narrows on both sides from the second shot on, so playing it safe is wise. Long hitters can reach the green in two.

=== Hole 9 ===
A par-4 with a wide fairway. The approach plays uphill, so choose your club carefully.

=== Hole 10 ===
A par-4 dogleg left. Aim the tee shot at the left hillside. The approach plays downhill — judge the distance and club choice carefully.

=== Hole 11 ===
A downhill par-3. Both sides of the green and the area behind it are tight — attacking from short of the green is the play.

=== Hole 12 ===
An uphill par-4. The tee shot from the front tee isn't difficult, but the approach plays uphill — choose your club carefully.

=== Hole 13 ===
A par-5 dogleg left. The entire left side is OB, so aim the tee shot at the centre. A pond guards the left of the green, so play the second shot safely down the centre and decide the hole on your third.

=== Hole 14 ===
A downhill par-4. The valley sits left and a pond sits right, so aim at the right side of the two-tiered fairway. Take one extra club for the approach.

=== Hole 15 ===
An uphill par-3. The green is hidden from the tee, making distance tough to judge. Aiming at the left side works well. Beware the large bunker fronting the green.

=== Hole 16 ===
A short par-4. Both sides of the fairway are tight, so a reliable tee shot is essential. The area behind the green is also tight, so beware.

=== Hole 17 ===
Beware the pond right of the tee shot landing area. Aiming just left of centre works well. The approach plays uphill, so choose your club carefully.

=== Hole 18 ===
A par-5 dogleg right. Aim the tee shot just left of fairway centre. Before your second shot, check the position of the group ahead to avoid hitting into them. With a pond on the right, aiming a touch left is the play.
