## Smoke evidence — what actually happened, step by step

Driven through the real entry path only: splash `StartButton` → bottom-nav `NavGachaButton` →
the banner card's own `PullX1Button` / `PullX10Button` → the Prizes screen's own `PullButton` and
the modal's own `SkipButton`. No synthetic buttons, no `ShowScreen` shortcuts.

- **A — enter.** The screen darkens to about half brightness including both persistent bars, the
  framed navy panel appears, and the bag scales up from 0.6 with an ease-out-back overshoot and
  settles. `GachaBagDrop` fires at t=0. Measured 0.32–0.35 s.
- **B — shake.** The bag rocks about its bottom edge, amplitude visibly growing from a twitch to a
  clear ±7° rock while the frequency rises; on Rare+ a rarity-tinted glow blooms behind it and the
  12-spoke ray sprite fades in and rotates slowly. `GachaBagShake`. 1.10 s on the first Legendary
  card, 0.60 s on subsequent ones, 0.35 s for Commons.
- **C — pop.** A puff of sparks bursts from the bag mouth and the card launches out of it, rising
  along a slight sideways arc (alternating side per card), scaling 0.25 → past 1 → 1 and fading in
  over the first 30 %, while the bag squashes to 0.94 on Y and springs back. `GachaCardPop`. 0.44 s.
- **D — land.** A radial spark burst fires from the card, the card's own ray fan fades in behind it,
  ambient sparkles start looping around it, and on Legendary+ tinted glitter falls through the card
  from the panel top. `GachaCardLand` plus the tier stinger — Commons get the land ding only, which
  is audible in the trace as four consecutive `CardLand` with no stinger after them.
- **E — hold.** The card rests; a tap anywhere ends the hold immediately (measured: a 2.00 s
  Legendary hold cut to 0.32 s), a tap during the pop does nothing.
- **F — exit.** The card shrinks to 0.6, drifts up 60 px and fades out; rays, glitter and the bag
  aura fade with it, so the next card builds its own aura from zero. `GachaCardExit`. 0.25 s.
- **G — finish.** After the last hold, `GachaRevealComplete` plays, the Prizes screen binds and
  activates *underneath* the still-opaque scrim, and the modal's 0.2 s fade reveals it with the
  cards popping in staggered 45 ms apart.

## Deviations from the SPEC

1. **Two new particle materials.** SPEC §4 names `Assets/Prefabs/UI/TapSparkle_Additive.mat` as the
   material for sparks. That material has **no texture** (`mainTexture=NULL`) — it renders plain
   additive quads, which is why the existing tap sparkles are soft squares. Using it would have left
   the spec's own `S_Gacha_Spark.png` and `S_Gacha_Confetti.png` unused. I added
   `M_Gacha_Spark_Additive.mat` and `M_Gacha_Confetti_Additive.mat`: same `UI/Additive` shader, with
   the authored sprite as `_MainTex`.
2. **`Panel/RevealPanel` is not in the SPEC's element table.** Added on Cesar's mid-build
   instruction; it *is* in the Figma frame. Measured off the reference render, cloned from the
   shipping `WrapPanel`.
3. **`Panel/TapCatcher` is not in the SPEC hierarchy.** The SPEC puts tap-to-fast-forward on the
   Scrim's Button, but the card's own graphics sit above the scrim and swallow taps on the card —
   and the SPEC also says the card should accept the tap. A transparent full-screen catcher inside
   the panel, above the card and below SKIP, satisfies both. The Scrim Button is still wired.
4. **`ParticleSystem.scalingMode = Local`, not `Hierarchy`.** SPEC §4 says Hierarchy; the cited
   precedent (`TapFeedbackFX.prefab`, which the same paragraph says to follow) uses Local and works.
   Chose the working precedent.
5. **No `ButtonPressFeedback` on the Scrim / TapCatcher Buttons.** Both are invisible full-screen
   input surfaces; scaling them on press would be a visual bug. The one real player-facing button,
   SKIP, has it (inherited with the clone).
6. **`--keep-audio` added to `Docs/Scripts/build_bot_video.py`.** The tool hard-stripped audio
   (`-an`) because no prior recorder captured any. Opt-in flag; default behaviour unchanged.
7. **`GachaRevealDemoRecorder.cs` and `gen_gacha_fx_sprites.py` are new files** beyond the SPEC's
   list — the sanctioned DemoRecorder pattern for evidence, and a reproducible generator so the FX
   sprites aren't mystery binaries.
8. **The shared binder now hides the card's action row.** Not cosmetic tidying: the Prizes grid
   cards ship with `LevelUpBtn`/`RepairBtn`/`SwapBtn` deactivated in the prefab, while a fresh
   `BagClubCard` instance has them active — so the same prize rendered two different ways depending
   on where it appeared. Hiding them in the one shared binder is what makes them agree, and it
   matches the Figma reveal card.

## Needs manual / on-device verification

Everything above was verified in Unity; per the standing rule no device pass is required. The two
things a human still has to judge:

1. **Whether the reveal *feels* right** — pacing, whether an x10 at 23.6 s is too long, and whether
   the rarity escalation reads as exciting. Every duration is a `[SerializeField]` on the modal and
   the whole `RarityFxTier` table is Inspector-tunable.
2. **The placeholder audio.** The 12 CC0 clips are mapped and fire in the right order at the right
   moments, but whether they *sound* like a gacha reveal is a taste call. Swapping a clip is a
   one-field change in `SfxLibrary.asset`.
