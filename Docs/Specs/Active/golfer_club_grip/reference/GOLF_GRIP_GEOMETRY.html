Golf Grip Geometry

Where the shaft actually lies in each hand, in landmarks a rig can measure. Right-handed golfer; lead = left hand, trail = right hand. Real-world sizes — scale by s = characterHeight / 1.75 (Remy stand-in: 1.328 m → s ≈ 0.76).

The one fact the rig has been missing: the club is not held across the palm like a bat. In the lead hand it runs diagonally through the fingers — from the base of the little finger to the middle joint of the index finger — with the heel pad of the hand sitting on top of the grip. A line fitted through the middle of a closed fist (what §3.8.3 asked for) gives a bat grip, rotated ~25–30° from a golf grip and shifted toward the wrist.
