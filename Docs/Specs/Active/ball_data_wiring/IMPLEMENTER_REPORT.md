### E. Perceptibility numbers per §4.2 — **PASS (reported, no threshold invented)**

Method, following `ball_rebound_perceptibility`: `BallSimulation.Simulate` on `FlatGround` +
`ConstantSurfaceProvider(Fairway)`, power 1.0 (70 m/s @ 12°, straight down +Z), gusts off, seed 7 —
but with a **fixed pure crosswind along +X** instead of calm, per §4.2. Only
`BallWindCutPerPoint` varies. `WindCutMax` = 0.30 throughout.

**§4.2's exact ask — Wind −10 / 0 / +10, before (0.01) and after (0.02), at an 8 m/s crosswind:**

| coeff | Wind | `windCutFraction` | lateral X (m) | carry Z (m) | total (m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | −10 | 0.0000 | 9.44 | 155.88 | 156.16 |
| 0.01 | 0 | 0.0000 | 9.44 | 155.88 | 156.16 |
| 0.01 | +10 | 0.0999 | **8.46** | 155.98 | 156.21 |
| 0.02 | −10 | 0.0000 | 9.44 | 155.88 | 156.16 |
| 0.02 | 0 | 0.0000 | 9.44 | 155.88 | 156.16 |
| 0.02 | +10 | 0.2000 | **7.48** | 156.08 | 156.26 |

So at 8 m/s of crosswind, a max-Wind ball recovers **0.98 m** of lateral push at 0.01 and
**1.96 m** at 0.02 — exactly double, as the linear coefficient implies. Carry is essentially
unaffected (+0.10 m → +0.20 m): this is a lateral-only lane.

**Wind −10 is bit-identical to Wind 0 at every coefficient.** `windCutFraction` is clamped to
`[0, WindCutMax]`, so a negative Wind stat has never done anything and still does nothing. The
change moves the top of the lane only. Worth knowing when reading the catalog: the six balls with
negative `windResistance` (`ball_ace_attire` −4, `ball_cirq` −4, `ball_par_perfect` is +3, etc.)
are not being *penalised* in wind — they are simply not being helped.

**Wider sweep, so the 0.02-vs-0.03 call is yours to make with numbers.** Lateral push recovered at
Wind +10 vs Wind 0:

| crosswind | @0.01 | @0.02 | @0.03 | lateral @Wind 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 m/s | 0.48 m | 0.95 m | 1.42 m | 4.61 m |
| 8 m/s | 0.98 m | 1.96 m | 2.92 m | 9.44 m |
| 12 m/s | 1.54 m | 3.05 m | 4.55 m | 14.48 m |
| 16 m/s | 2.14 m | 4.24 m | 6.30 m | 19.76 m |

0.03 is the value at which +10 Wind exactly saturates the 0.30 `WindCutMax` cap — the same
"fills its cap band at ±10" shape Rebound (Order 417) and Roll (F8) already have. At 0.02 a
max-Wind ball claims two thirds of the cap.

**No pass threshold is asserted.** Order 417's bar was 10 m of *distance* and does not transfer to a
lateral effect; §4.2 says wind has no agreed bar. These are the measurements — the call on 0.02 vs
0.03 is yours.
