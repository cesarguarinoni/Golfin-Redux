## Implementation summary

**F9 (the Order 731 change) — DONE and proven.** Retired the duplicate resolver-side stamina multiplier lane and the off-design ClubControl aim-cone term from `StatModifierResolver.Resolve`. Both deleted lanes are no-ops on the FALLBACK path (proven bit-identical by `fallback_bitidentity_proof.txt`). Three unit tests updated + one added; physics suite green (234 pass / 0 fail in `Golfin.Physics.Tests`, re-run 2026-07-17). F9 entry landed in `PHYSICS_TUNING_CHANGELOG.md`.

**Putter-from-Fairway bug — root-caused and FIXED (scoped exception, Cesar 2026-07-16).** A putt fired from a non-putt surface (Fairway) fails `IsPutt`'s surface gate and is misrouted to `SimulateAirborne`, whose origin sits on/below the sim ground so the airborne ground-crossing guard (`BallSimulation.cs:533`) never fires → the ball free-falls to y≈-1582m. The real game (`PutterModeSurfaceController`) and `VersusBot` (H3b) already prevent off-green putts; **BotDriver was the lone harness that didn't**. Fixed bot-side only (off-green putter guard → chip), **zero deterministic-physics change** — the F9 bit-identity is untouched.

**LIVE-path club switch — root-caused and FIXED.** BotDriver's `SetClub` only sets the LAB club index; the LiveStatProvider resolves the swing club from `ClubContext.SelectedClubId` (`LiveStatProviderHost.cs:188`), which `SetClub` never touched — so every shot fired the equipped **driver** (clubVel=75, loft=10.9°) and overshot ~2×. BotDriver now drives `ClubContext.SelectedClubId` to the nearest available equipped club (the default bag has no wedge → approaches resolve to the Iron). This took convergence from map-wide oscillation to **<1m from the cup**.

**DoD #3 (≤7-stroke completion video) — WAIVED by Cesar (2026-07-17).** With the two fixes above the bot reaches <1m from the cup but cannot cleanly hole out, blocked by **pre-existing bot-harness issues orthogonal to F9**: (A) the aim "shoots straight" / the flag-tail (`HoleIndicatorWidget`) does not point at the pin (likely the SHOT_CONTROLS_DESIGN §3.4 arrow-sign drift; F9 only changed dispersion *width*, and the `ClubContext` event only refreshes `ClubButtonWidget` — neither touches the flag/aim path), and (B) Hole 1's green sits ~5.6m below the surrounding fairway so approaches land on the lip (endSurface=Fairway even 0.8m out) and never settle on the green to putt. F9 is provably safe (FALLBACK bit-identical; LIVE = stamina no-op at match-start + minor cone), so the completability check is satisfied by proof. The remaining bot rehab is filed as a **separate order** (spawn_task `task_99aeae55`).

### DoD #3 — Circuit breaker: putter-from-Fairway underground bug (5 attempts, same root cause)

**Root cause.** The Hole 1 cup is at approximately `(-9.0, 6.82, 41.0)` world-units. After a full-power Driver tee shot and one or two wedge approach shots, the ball lands on a Fairway slope ABOVE the green. After the chip shot (stroke 3), the ball ends at `(-7.5, 12.3, 40.4)` — only 1.6m lateral distance from the cup but 5.5m above it in elevation. BotDriver selects Putter (correct for the distance). The putter fires from Fairway surface rather than Green surface; putter physics sends the ball to y≈-1582m underground. `OnShotComplete` is never raised within the 32.1s bot timeout; the bot reads the animator's current position (underground) as the next-stroke origin. The cycle repeats, going deeper each stroke.
